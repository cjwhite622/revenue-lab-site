Before you send: evidence checklist


  • Dated check-in and check-out photos of the same spots (wide shot, then close-up).
  • Receipts or quotes for each repair or replacement. Estimates are weaker than invoices.
  • The house rules and booking confirmation the guest agreed to.
  • Your message history with the guest, unedited.
  • Charge for actual loss only. Normal wear and tear and the age of an item usually reduce what you can recover.
  • Send within the platform's claim window; check how many days you have.
