What to include in pet sitter instructions

A good sheet answers the questions a sitter would otherwise text you about: how much food and when, which medications and how to give them, the daily routine, who to call in an emergency, and which vet to go to. Add anything that keeps your pet safe: escape risks (doors, gates), fears, and foods or plants to keep away.

Emergency details matter most

Write down your regular vet and the nearest 24-hour emergency clinic, a backup contact who can make decisions if you can't be reached, and how much you authorize the sitter to spend on emergency care. Include the microchip number so a lost pet can be reported quickly.
