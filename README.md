## Handling scope creep in one email
Acknowledge the request, price it, offer two options (add it at $X, or swap it for something already in scope), and ask for a yes in writing.

## Chasing a late invoice
Day 1 friendly reminder, day 7 firm reminder with the late fee, day 14 final notice, then a pause on work.

Estimates only, not legal or tax advice.
