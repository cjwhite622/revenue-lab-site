Does a pet GPS tracker need a subscription?

Almost always, yes for trackers that show a live location on your phone from anywhere. Those devices contain a SIM card and send their position over mobile networks, and the plan covers that data connection.

What the fee pays for

Tractive says its trackers have a built-in SIM card, like a phone, that uses mobile networks to send location, and that all the fees for this are covered by the subscription (source: Tractive's dog tracker page, checked 2026-10-21). It also states that a device needs 2G and/or LTE cellular coverage from a partner network to connect.

Why a Bluetooth-only tag is different

Item tags that rely on Bluetooth have a short direct range and, for wider search, rely on other people's phones. They usually have no monthly fee but are not the same product as a cellular GPS tracker. Check each maker's description for what network it uses.

How to compare plans

  1. Write down the device price and every plan length offered (monthly, 1-year, multi-year).
  2. Check which features each tier includes (location history length, health monitoring, family sharing).
  3. Use the GPS tracker cost calculator to see the 1-, 3- and 5-year totals.
  4. Check cancellation and refund terms before paying for a long plan.

Plan names and prices change often and vary by country. Confirm on the maker's page.
