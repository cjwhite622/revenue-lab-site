Thanks! Your Freelancer Client Kit

Copy, paste, replace the [BRACKETS]. Bookmark this page. Not legal advice; have a lawyer review contracts for large projects.

1. Project proposal

Subject: Proposal: [PROJECT NAME]

Hi [CLIENT],

Thanks for talking with me. Here is what I understood and how I'd approach it.

GOAL: [one sentence on the result the client wants]
DELIVERABLES: [bullet list, concrete and countable]
NOT INCLUDED: [things that are out of scope]
TIMELINE: [start date] to [end date], with check-ins on [days]
PRICE: [$X] fixed, or [$Y/hour] capped at [Z] hours
PAYMENT: 50% to start, 50% on delivery. Invoices due in 14 days.
NEXT STEP: Reply "approved" and I'll send the agreement and first invoice.

This proposal is valid for 14 days.
[YOUR NAME]

2. Scope-of-work agreement

This agreement is between [YOUR NAME] ("Freelancer") and [CLIENT] ("Client").

1. Work: Freelancer will deliver [DELIVERABLES] as described in the proposal dated [DATE].
2. Revisions: [2] rounds of revisions are included. Extra rounds are billed at [$/hour].
3. Changes: Work outside this scope needs written approval and may change price and timeline.
4. Fees and payment: [$X], [50%] due before work starts, balance due [14] days after delivery.
5. Late payment: Balances unpaid after [14] days may incur [1.5%] monthly interest. Work may pause until paid.
6. Client responsibilities: Client provides content, feedback and approvals within [3] business days. Delays may move the timeline.
7. Ownership: On full payment, Client owns the final deliverables. Freelancer may show the work in a portfolio unless Client objects in writing.
8. Confidentiality: Each party keeps the other's non-public information private.
9. Ending early: Either party may end this agreement with [7] days written notice. Client pays for work completed to that date.
10. Liability: Freelancer's liability is limited to the fees paid under this agreement.

Signed: ____________ [Freelancer]   Date: ______
Signed: ____________ [Client]       Date: ______

3. Discovery call questionnaire

- What problem are we solving, and why now?
- What does success look like in 3 months? How will you measure it?
- Who approves the work, and who gives feedback?
- What have you already tried?
- What is your deadline, and what drives it?
- What budget range have you set aside?
- What would make you regret hiring me?
- What do you need from me that is not in the brief?
- Anything I should know about past freelancers or agencies?

4. Late-invoice email sequence

DAY 1 AFTER DUE
Subject: Quick check on invoice [#]
Hi [CLIENT], a friendly note that invoice [#] for [$AMOUNT] was due on [DATE]. Payment link: [LINK]. If it's already on its way, thank you!

DAY 7
Subject: Invoice [#] is a week overdue
Hi [CLIENT], I haven't received payment for invoice [#] ([$AMOUNT]). Can you confirm when I can expect it? If there's an issue with the invoice, tell me and I'll fix it today.

DAY 14
Subject: Action needed: invoice [#]
Hi [CLIENT], invoice [#] is now 14 days overdue. Per our agreement, work pauses and late fees begin on [DATE] unless payment arrives. I'd rather keep things moving. Can you pay by [DATE]?

If you'd like to schedule upcoming work at the current rate, send me the details by [DATE] and I'll lock it in.

Thanks for the trust,
[YOUR NAME]
