About Pet Tech Guide and how we work

Pet Tech Guide is a small independent site that helps people see the real cost of pet technology.

What we do

We build calculators and explainers from the makers' published prices and specifications, dated and linked where possible. Numbers that come from a third party instead of the maker are labelled as such.

What we do not do

We do not buy, own or test these devices, and we do not publish hands-on reviews or star ratings. If a page ever contains our own testing, it will say what was tested and how.

Money

There are currently no ads and no affiliate links. If we add affiliate links in future, each page that has them will say so near the top, and they will not change the calculations.

Corrections

Prices change and we can be wrong. Treat every figure as a starting point and confirm on the seller's page before buying.
