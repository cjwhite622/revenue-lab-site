How to use this

Check the maker's page for the price of replacement liners, filters or cartridges, because that recurring cost often decides which box is cheaper. If you have more than one cat, raise the litter figure. Also weigh non-cost factors (noise, cleaning effort, reliability reports) that a calculator cannot capture.
