Where social media managers lose money

  • The pitfall: Community replies and reporting eat unbilled hours.
  • The fix: Define posts per week, reply windows and report cadence in the package.
  • Scope creep to expect: Requests for extra platforms or campaigns on the same retainer.

Frequently asked questions

How do I set my hourly rate as a freelance social media manager?
Start from the take-home income you want, add tax and business costs to get the revenue you need, then divide by the hours you can realistically bill in a year. This calculator does that for you.

How many billable hours a week should a social media manager plan for?
Far fewer than a full week. Sales, admin, revisions and gaps between projects are unbilled. 26 hours a week is used here as an editable starting point, not a market statistic.

Should I charge hourly or per project?
Use the hourly figure as your floor, then quote fixed prices that clear it with room for revisions. If a project takes longer than planned, the margin protects your real hourly pay.
